# OctoAcme Project Management

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Each initiative begins with a one-pager that defines the problem, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, resource needs, and a high-level timeline. Approved initiatives are developed into prioritized backlogs of small, shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a shared Definition of Done.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, documentation, and stakeholder communication. Product Managers own product outcomes, roadmap priorities, and success metrics, while developers design, build, test, document, and review solutions. QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input, approvals, and escalation support.

Teams maintain transparency through project boards and a shared source of truth for status. Their communication rhythm includes standups, weekly delivery and product alignment meetings, sprint or milestone demos, and regular stakeholder updates. Risks have named owners, mitigation plans, and weekly reviews, with clear escalation paths for blockers and incidents.

Quality is built into delivery through small reviewed pull requests, automated tests, linting, security scans, and manual acceptance testing where needed. Releases require passing checks, rollback plans, staging smoke tests, production verification, and stakeholder announcements. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents turn lessons into owned, measurable improvement actions.
